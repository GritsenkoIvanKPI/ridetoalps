The drive

Munich Airport to Munich, 40 km

The airport sits about 30 km north-east of the city, and the drive is almost entirely motorway: the A92 west, then the A99 ring road south before dropping into town. Outside rush hour it is a comfortable 40 minutes. Between roughly 07:00 and 09:30, and again from 16:00 to 18:30, the A9 into the centre can add twenty minutes or more, so we build that into the plan rather than leaving you watching the clock.

No mountain passes, no tolls, no seasonal closures — this is the one route that is the same in January and July.
Bavaria, Germany

About Munich

Munich rewards an early start. The Altstadt is compact enough to walk end to end in half an hour, from Marienplatz and the Viktualienmarkt to the Hofbräuhaus and the Residenz. If you are connecting onward to the Alps the next morning, staying near the Hauptbahnhof or Sendlinger Tor keeps everything close. Many of our guests use this transfer as the first or last leg of a longer trip — airport to hotel on arrival, hotel to airport on the way home, same fixed price in both directions.
