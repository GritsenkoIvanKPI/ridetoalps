  • Besondere Wünsche: Alter und Gewicht eines Kindes zur Auswahl des richtigen Kindersitzes, Mitnahme von Tieren, zusätzliche Halte auf der Strecke
  • Abweichendes Ziel: die von Ihnen eingegebene Ortsangabe, wenn Sie „Other destination“ wählen

Zweck: Erstellung eines Angebots, Abschluss und Durchführung des Beförderungsvertrags, Kommunikation vor und während der Fahrt.
Rechtsgrundlage: Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO (Vertragserfüllung bzw. vorvertragliche Maßnahmen auf Ihre Anfrage hin).
Bereitstellung: Die Angaben zu Kontakt, Strecke und Zeitpunkt sind für eine Buchung erforderlich; ohne sie können wir den Transfer nicht durchführen. Alle übrigen Angaben sind freiwillig.

Booking enquiries

When you submit an enquiry we process your name, telephone or WhatsApp number and email address, together with your route, date and time, any return leg, the number of passengers, ski or board bags and other luggage, your flight number where you provide it, and any special requirements such as a child's age and weight for a child seat, pets, or additional stops. If you choose "Other destination" we also process the location you type in.

The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR — performance of a contract or pre-contractual steps taken at your request. Contact, route and timing details are necessary for a booking; without them we cannot carry out the transfer. All other details are optional. Your data is passed only to the driver assigned to your journey, limited to what that journey requires.

WhatsApp, telephone and email

If you contact us via WhatsApp, your number, message content and communication metadata are processed by WhatsApp Ireland Limited, Merrion Road, Dublin 4, Ireland, and may be transferred to Meta servers in the United States on the basis of the EU Commission's standard contractual clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework adequacy decision. We have no influence over that processing. The link on this site only opens WhatsApp once you actively click it; no data is sent beforehand. If you would rather avoid this transfer, please use telephone or email.

Payment and newsletter

A deposit of €30 or $35 confirms a booking; the balance is paid after the ride. Payment data is passed to payment provider to be named on the basis of Article 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR. Newsletter sign-up is based on your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and can be withdrawn at any time.

Retention

Enquiries that do not lead to a booking are deleted once the matter is closed. Invoices and booking records are kept for up to ten years to satisfy Section 147 of the German Fiscal Code and Section 257 of the German Commercial Code. Newsletter data is kept until you withdraw consent.
